Student Work & Experiments

Explore the visual logs of our school hydroponics experiment and test out our custom diagnostic model with actual dataset leaves.

Classroom Greenhouse Log

Over a 30-day monitoring block, our student team grew butterhead lettuce inside a mock hydroponics NFT setup using recycled PVC gutter channels and an electric low-voltage water pump.

We tracked two core groups under identical LED lighting structures: an experimental NFT setup receiving automated liquid nutrients, and a control group planted in high-quality garden soil. We photographed the leaves daily to build a dataset for training our leaf disease classifier model.

Growth Analysis (30-Day Period)
